Correction:
The old extra 180-degree visual roll, combined with swapped source hands, showed
palmar surfaces with an incompatible first-person handedness. Keep the original
FBX conversion and anatomical Left/Right models. In first person, dorsal surfaces
face the camera, thumbs point inward, and fingertips point upward.

Verification checks UV-identified palmar/dorsal surface normals and thumb geometry,
rather than trusting Euler angles or the labels of the exported objects.
